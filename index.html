Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2026

Overview

CashTrack is a personal budgeting application designed to help users track expenses, income, subscriptions, and financial habits. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about how the app works.

Data You Enter

All financial data you enter into CashTrack — including expenses, income, subscriptions, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any external server or third party.

Your data stays on your device No accounts required. No cloud sync. No data leaves your phone.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

CashTrack uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized, aggregated usage data. This helps us understand how the app is used and improve the experience over time.

Firebase Analytics

The following anonymous data may be collected:

  • App open and session duration events
  • General feature usage patterns
  • Device type, OS version, and country
  • App version and crash reports

This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally. It is processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

Internet Usage

The app uses internet access only for the following limited purposes:

  • Loading Google Fonts for visual design
  • Sending anonymized analytics events via Firebase
  • Standard Android system services required by the device

Device Sensors

CashTrack optionally uses the device accelerometer and gyroscope to enable motion-based screen switching. This sensor data is processed locally in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

Notifications

The app uses local on-device notifications to remind you to log expenses or stay on track with your budget. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers.

Advertising

CashTrack contains no advertisements and does not use any advertising networks or tracking tools of any kind.

Data Deletion

All locally stored app data can be permanently deleted at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Children's Privacy

CashTrack does not knowingly collect personal data from users of any age. No personally identifiable information is collected through the app.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions of CashTrack to reflect changes or improvements to the app. Continued use of the app following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
