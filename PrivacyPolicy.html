Privacy Policy

CashTrack
Effective Date: April 30, 2026

1. Overview

CashTrack is a budgeting application designed to help users track expenses, income, subscriptions, and financial habits directly on their device.

2. Data Collection

CashTrack does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All user data remains exclusively on the user’s device.

3. Data Storage

All financial data entered into CashTrack is stored locally on the device only. This includes expenses, income entries, subscriptions, and user preferences.

4. Internet Usage

The app uses internet access only for limited technical purposes:

  • Loading Google Fonts for improved visual design
  • Standard Android system services required by the device

No user data is transmitted over the internet at any time.

5. Device Sensors

CashTrack uses the device’s accelerometer and gyroscope solely to enable optional motion-based screen switching. This data is processed locally in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

6. Notifications

The app uses local notifications to remind users to log expenses or track financial activity. Notifications are generated on-device and do not involve external servers.

7. Third-Party Services

CashTrack does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking tools, or external data services.

8. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally, security depends on the user’s device security settings. Users are responsible for protecting their device and any stored data.

9. Data Deletion

All app data can be permanently deleted by clearing the app data or uninstalling the application.

10. Children’s Privacy

CashTrack does not knowingly collect any personal data from users of any age.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions of CashTrack to reflect app changes or improvements.
